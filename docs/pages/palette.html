seqfu palette

18 colours in four tiers. Each swatch shows the text colour to put on it and the WCAG contrast ratio. Click a hex code to copy it.

Tier 1Primary

The three colours of the logo. Purple carries the brand, orange marks evidence, black gives depth.

Tier 2Accessory shades

Lighter and darker steps of the two brand hues, for tints, borders and coloured text. Shades only, never blended into gradients.

Tier 3Neutrals and status

Backgrounds, borders and secondary text; status colours always come with a word.

Tier 4Sequence

For nucleotide letters only, never for UI. The letter carries the meaning; colour is a second cue.

Rules of thumb

  • White on seqfu-purple; black on seqfu-orange. Never orange text on light grounds.
  • Orange on purple only at 24px and above, as in the wordmark.
  • One orange highlight per view: the number, match or new thing that matters.
  • Flat fills only: no gradients, glows or blends between colours.
